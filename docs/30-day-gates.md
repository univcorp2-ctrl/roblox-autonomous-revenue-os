# 30-day commercial gates

This is a kill-or-scale system, not a build-at-all-costs roadmap.

## Plugin gate

Scale only when all are true:
- at least 5 paid sales in the first meaningful listing test window,
- support burden below 10 minutes per sale,
- install-to-first-value under 3 minutes,
- refund/critical-defect rate below 5%,
- at least one repeated customer request cluster worth a paid adjacent product.

## Experience gate

Do not buy traffic until organic/on-platform tests show:
- tutorial completion >= 70%,
- first-session core loop completion >= 60%,
- D1 retention trend is improving rather than merely session time,
- social interaction is additive rather than required friction,
- monetization prompts do not reduce retention in canary tests.

## Kill rules

Kill or radically reposition when two consecutive well-instrumented iterations fail to move the target KPI. Never respond to weak retention by simply adding more content or more monetization prompts.
